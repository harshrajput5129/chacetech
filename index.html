Designed for Builders and Custom Homes

We design and install smart home automation, audio/video systems, networking, and camera infrastructure during construction so your home is ready for the future from day one.

Early Stage Planning

We work with your blueprints to design the low-voltage infrastructure before framing begins.

Pre-Drywall Installation

All cabling and rough-ins are completed cleanly and inspected before the drywall goes up.

No Retrofitting Required

By planning ahead, we eliminate the need to cut into finished walls or compromise the home's design.

The Problem With Afterthought Technology

Most smart home systems are installed after the drywall is up. This leads to compromised aesthetics, unreliable wireless connections, and limited expandability.

Visible wires and bulky equipment cluttering your luxury space

Unreliable Wi-Fi struggling to penetrate modern building materials

Disjointed apps that don't communicate with each other

Costly and messy retrofitting when you want to upgrade

ChaceTech AV solves this by integrating technology during the build phase. We work alongside your builder to ensure perfect execution.

Smart Home Technology Done Right

Our proven 3-step process ensures your home's technology is as reliable as its foundation.

01

Consultation & Planning

We collaborate with you and your architect
during the design phase to map out your
technology needs before construction begins.

02

Infrastructure Installation

03

System Integration

After finishing, we install the visible hardware
and program a seamless automation system for
