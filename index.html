The Problem With Afterthought Technology

Most smart home systems are installed after the drywall is up. This leads to compromised aesthetics, unreliable wireless connections, and limited expandability.

Visible wires and bulky equipment cluttering your luxury space

Unreliable Wi-Fi struggling to penetrate modern building materials

Disjointed apps that don't communicate with each other

Costly and messy retrofitting when you want to upgrade

ChaceTech AV solves this by integrating technology during the build phase. We work alongside your builder to ensure perfect execution.

Smart Home Technology Done Right

Our proven 3-step process ensures your home's technology is as reliable as its foundation.

01

Consultation & Planning

We collaborate with you and your architect during the design phase to map out your technology needs before construction begins.

02

Infrastructure Installation

Our technicians install enterprise-grade cabling, networking, and hardware backbones while the walls are still open.

03

System Integration

After finishing, we install the visible hardware and program a seamless automation system for lighting, AV, and security.
